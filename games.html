>Danganronpa: Salvation's Fears

Danganronpa: Salvation's Fears is an actively developing fangan in RPGMakerMV. You primarily play as Nagamori Saemonsaburu, the Ultimate Hero and his sister, Mari as he tries to save as many of his 15 fellow classmates as possible from a killing game run by a killer jukebox. The project features fully voice acted trials, CGs and more.

Danganronpa: Salvation's Fears uses Fangan Maker from Chapter 4 onwards.
